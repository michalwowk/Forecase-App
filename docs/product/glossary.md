# Glossary

- **Place** — a city or town resolved by geocoding to name, country, lat/lon, timezone.
- **Day** — a calendar day in the place's local timezone.
- **Activity** — one of: skiing, surfing, outdoor sightseeing, indoor sightseeing.
- **Score** — one comparable number per activity per day. Scale in `scoring.md`.
- **Reason** — short human text explaining a score.
- **Ranking** — the 7 days of one activity, ordered by score.
- **Change** — one unit of work: `docs/changes/NNNN-slug/` + branch `change/NNNN-slug`.
